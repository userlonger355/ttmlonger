# Additional independently adapted features

Based on inspection of the user-supplied MargyT archive, specifically Brightness.java and RepostPurge.java. The brightness option is off by default, intercepts only window brightness overrides, and does not claim to disable HDR video renditions. The repost screen uses version-specific native service signatures from the reference (47.2.41); it lists at most 150 entries and requires explicit selection and confirmation of up to 20. Account identity is checked again before every change. Unknown responses stop rather than retry. Actual signed-in compatibility with 47.9.3 is unverified; no account mutations are part of CI. No direct source import. Archive GPL-3.0 and additional terms are documented in previous compatibility notes. Likes and favorites remain pending, because the supplied archive does not establish verified native deletion signatures for them.

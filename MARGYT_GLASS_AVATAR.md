# Avatar and glass reference
MargyT archive: AvatarShape.java, GlassShader.java, GlassOptics.java, dexpatch.py. Implemented independently against the current Xposed architecture: geometric masks, scoped round-path substitutions and opt-in 9-second rotation with a 30 Hz invalidation limit. The reference's class anchors are version-specific; unresolved hooks leave native images unchanged. Story rings are not yet migrated. The optical changes use a bounded edge-decay curve, local touch magnification and increased drag damping in the existing TTMLonger shader/physics, not a wholesale copy of the GPL source. MargyT's optical upstream is QWEA0/Liquid-Glass-Android (MIT, copyright 2025–2026 pandadog). Real 47.9.3 feed and Android 17 remain unverified.
